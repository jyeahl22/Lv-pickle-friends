How it works

Three steps to your next game

No app to download yet — just a profile, a waitlist spot, and (soon) a way to see who's free this weekend.

01 — Free

Join the waitlist

Reserve your spot with just your name and email. No cost, no commitment — this is how we know how many players are actually out there.

02 — 2 min

Build your profile

Add your DUPR rating if you have one — or just pick a self-rated skill level if you don't — plus a short bio, your home courts, and age group so people know who they're playing.

03 — Ongoing

Meet players at your level

Browse Lehigh Valley players filtered by skill level — DUPR or self-rated — age group, and home court, then post "Looking to Play" to line up a date, time, and court in minutes.
